Questions

The fair questions

What is the catch?

There is not one. Some people who see their X-Ray ask us to help fix what it finds. Most do not, and that is fine. The X-Ray is yours either way.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. You will get the X-Ray on WhatsApp and email. If you want to talk it through, we will offer you 15 minutes. If not, keep the page and use it.

Will you contact my customers or competitors?

We send one ordinary enquiry to time how fast it gets answered, the same way any customer would. We do not contact your customers, and nothing we do is visible to your competitors.

Why do you ask about enquiries and spend?

So the rand figures are based on your business and not on our guesses. A rough range is enough. You can always correct the numbers when you see the page.

I already have a marketing agency.

Then the X-Ray is a useful second opinion. Plenty of people send it to their agency as a list of things to look at.
