Asked on almost every call

Fair questions, straight answers.

Do we have to change our website, CRM or listing platform?
No. Most agency sites run on a franchise or national platform you don't control, and most of their defects are not yours to fix. The engine sits behind what you already run, catches what those systems produce, and manages everything after that moment. Keep your stack. Fix the gap.
My agents already get the leads straight to their phones. Isn't that faster?
It's faster when the agent is at a desk, awake, and not with another client. It's invisible the rest of the time. The engine answers in under a minute every time, and your agent still gets the lead, already qualified, often with the viewing already booked. Agents lose the triage, not the commission.
What do agents see, and what do I see?
Each agent sees their own enquiries, their own pipeline, their own conversations, and nothing else. You see every office and every agent by name: who got the enquiry, how fast they answered, what they did next and what came of it. That view is yours alone.
Will the replies sound like a robot?
Read the conversation at the top of this page again. That is the standard: warm, specific to the listing, and asking useful questions. The engine is trained on your areas, your stock and your tone during the build, and we review real conversations with you in the first weeks and tune it. You approve the voice before it ever speaks to a buyer.
Do you work with our competitors?
We work with agencies across the country and we don't take exclusivity in a town by default. If exclusivity matters to you, say so and we will build it into the agreement. You'll always get that answer straight.
Is this POPIA-safe?
Materially safer than the status quo. Consent is captured at the point the buyer enquires, data lives in one system the agency controls instead of scattered across personal handsets, and every conversation is on the record. For most agencies this is the first time their lead data would survive an audit.
What happens if we stop?
After the diagnostic: you keep the audit and every number in it. At day ninety: if your numbers haven't moved, you stop and keep everything built. After that it's month to month. Nobody gets locked in a room.
